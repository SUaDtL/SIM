Q-001

Vision: what is SIM

questionuser-reportedanswered
AnswerService Integration Map. The user goes into struggling enterprises and helps them out of it by applying technology. These enterprises often span multiple network domains (e.g. SIPR, NIPR, DEV); each network has different users, services and purposes, even though it is the same overall group of people, and not everyone uses every service on every network. The first step is usually to inventory which services are provided or consumed on each network, who provides or consumes them, and who the Tier 1/2/3 support is. SIM records services (what the service is, which application provides it, which network it is on, a category such as cyber, ops or C2) and programmatically or dynamically maps the connections between services and networks into a single-pane view the user can pan around, highlight nodes or services in, and see everything that chains to a node. It is mainly used to show visually how complexity will be reduced by combining services, paths and so on. Visualization is 99% of the purpose.
