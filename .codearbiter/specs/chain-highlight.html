Chain highlight and filters

Clicking a node highlights everything that chains to it (upstream providers and downstream consumers, with CDS hops and foundational links) and fades the rest; the map can be filtered by network, category and organization without moving or writing anything.

Approach

APP-1

Approach

Binding decision refs
  • DEC-1
  • DEC-2
ChoicePure traceChain and filterGraph in graph.ts compute element sets from the dependency model (separate upstream and downstream walks; via-CDS routes kept apart). The view re-applies foundational, chain, dim and filter classes after every sync; stylesheet order makes a filter beat a chain and a chain beat the foundational toggle.
TradeoffWalking dependencies rather than Cytoscape edges avoids leaking through shared providers and shared CDS nodes; re-applying classes after every sync keeps view state through edits and drags at negligible cost.

Decisions

DEC-1

Chain contents

Authorityinherited
ChoiceTransitive upstream (providers of providers) and downstream (consumers of consumers) walked separately; a network starts from its services, a CDS from its routes; chains always show their foundational links.
RationaleCAP-005 and Q-021; mixing directions would pull every consumer of a shared provider such as Identity into every chain.
Source refSRC-DECOMP
DEC-2

Organization filter

Authorityinherited
ChoiceAn organization matches a service it provides, supports at any tier, or consumes (labelled in the tooltip).
RationaleQ-012: provides/consumes are both organization-to-service; the consultant's question is what an organization is involved in.

Sections

SEC-SMARTS

SMARTS design rationale

Blocks

Traversal: Cytoscape successors/predecessors versus a walk over the dependency model. The graph walk leaks across a CDS shared by unrelated routes and, if directions mix, through shared providers; the dependency walk is exact and unit-testable. Fading: element opacity versus fill/border/label opacity on networks; opacity on a compound fades its children, so networks fade through their own fill, border and label.

References: DEC-1
