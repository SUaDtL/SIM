PNG export

Export PNG saves the current map view at twice screen resolution, framed top and bottom by the dataset's classification banner.

Approach

APP-1

Approach

Binding decision refs
  • DEC-1
ChoiceCytoscape renders the current viewport at scale 2 on white; frameWithBanner draws banner bands above and below with the same colors as the app banner (BANNER_COLORS, checked against the CSS) and bannerText; the workspace downloads it as '<dataset> map.png'.
TradeoffExporting the viewport keeps zoom, highlight and filters as the user arranged them; full-graph export can follow with SVG (WORK-012).
