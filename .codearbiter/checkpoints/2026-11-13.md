### Research question and recruitment plan

Can the intended single-user enterprise consultant inventory a small multi-network estate and produce a trustworthy decision map without coaching or accidental data loss? Validate recognizability, repetitive entry, shared/copy boundaries, direction/uncertainty interpretation, and save/recovery trust. Do not use these sessions as evidence of willingness to pay or market demand.

Recruit three role cohorts: first-time domain-literate consultants; frequent service/data owners who do repeated entry; and architects/decision reviewers, including classification responsibilities. Recruit across Chrome/Edge, laptop sizes, and assistive-technology needs. Proposed initial exploratory round: 3–4 people per cohort, maximum 12, adjusted to actual availability. These are proposed counts, not a representative sample or completed participants. Evaluate each person individually before group discussion so group coaching does not contaminate task results.

### Session plan, consent, and setup

Plan a 60-minute individual moderated session plus a 30-minute role-group discussion. Select up to six relevant task cards per person; do not rush all thirteen. First-use cohort: 1/2/3/10/11/13; repeated-entry cohort: 2/3/4/5/6/11; decision-review cohort: 1/7/8/9/10/13. Use recovery task 12 in a separately prepared targeted session or replace a lower-priority card. Record unattempted tasks as not attempted. These are proposed times only.

Use a facilitator and observer. Invite note-taking/recording consent in advance; use participant IDs and synthetic UNCLASSIFIED fixtures. Participants may stop. No customer inventories, live classified data, or runtime telemetry. Retain notes/recordings only with a declared purpose, location, owner, and deletion date agreed beforehand.

Prepare the exact candidate HTML and SHA-256, a clean temporary profile in the supported browser, a synthetic empty dataset, the supplied 100-service teaching/stress dataset, temporary `.sqlite` targets, and a capture location. Include two same-name Identity services on different networks, an unknown/no-CDS route, one provider/support organization, and a scenario. Verify the artifact before the session. Do not install dependencies or ask participants to build software. Give a task card and hide moderator oracles. Do not give README guidance before the first attempt. ### Capture and discussion

Capture per task: participant ID/role/browser/viewport/artifact SHA; independent completion, assisted completion, failure, or not attempted; exact resulting dataset/capture evidence; wrong turns; mistaken assumptions; error recovery; assistance and reason; observed elapsed time from the actual session only; and participant confidence in their own words with consent. Keep qualitative observations distinct from task outcomes and never invent quotes or statistics. A moderator hint makes the outcome assisted. A failure discovered by the participant still counts and is useful.

Hold group discussion after individual tasks. Ask what participants expected before their first action; which terms they reinterpreted; where shared versus copied records were unclear; when they trusted Saved/recovery; whether the map could support a real decision; what was missing from standalone exports; what would block adoption; and which aesthetic choices helped/hurt. Probe specific task evidence rather than a show-of-hands popularity vote. A focus group cannot replace observed individual task completion.
